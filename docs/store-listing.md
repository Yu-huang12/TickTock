## Full description (Google Play max 4000 chars / Apple description)

```
Think you can feel exactly when 5 seconds have passed? Tick Tock Challenge is the addictive
party game that tests your internal stopwatch — the clock stays hidden, so you have to trust
your gut.

Each round shows a target time. Start the timer, count in your head, and stop as close to the
target as you can. The closer you land, the higher your tier — Perfect, Great, Good — and the
more points you score.

GAME MODES
• Solo — chase targets, build streaks, and track your accuracy over time.
• Online Multiplayer — play with friends on their own phones. Create a room and share a
  4-letter code, or just scan the QR. Everyone races the same targets with a live leaderboard.
• Pass & Play — hand one device around the table for instant local multiplayer. Works offline.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Dead-simple to learn, surprisingly hard to master.
• Fast rounds — perfect for a quick break or a long game night.
• No accounts, no sign-ups. Jump straight in.
• No ads and no tracking.
• Tap or hit the spacebar — your call.

Whether you're settling a "who has the best timing" debate or just killing five minutes,
Tick Tock Challenge turns the simple act of counting seconds into something weirdly competitive.

Trust your inner clock. ⏱️
```

## Data safety / App privacy answers

Use these when filling out Apple's "App Privacy" and Google Play's "Data safety" forms.

- **Does the app collect data?** Minimal.
  - Anonymous identifier — used for app functionality (matchmaking), not linked to identity, not
    used for tracking.
  - User content — the display name/nickname the player types, shown to other players.
- **Is data used to track you across apps/sites?** No.
- **Is data shared with third parties?** Only the backend provider (Supabase) to operate online
  play. No advertisers or data brokers.
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS/WSS).
- **Camera:** Used only to scan QR codes to join a room; images are not stored or transmitted.
- **Account required?** No. No login, email, or password.
